[h1]Deposit Refill Every 10 Sols[/h1]

For Surviving Mars: Relaunched.

Each new Sol, resource deposits that are below their max amount are filled back up (Metals, Rare Metals, Water, Concrete, and other deposit types the game tracks). Deposits already full are left alone.

The title says 10 Sols. The current script runs once per Sol.

[h1]Source[/h1]
Public GitHub, MIT license, fork and edit freely:
[url=https://github.com/khunpoom/DepositRefillEvery10Sols]https://github.com/khunpoom/DepositRefillEvery10Sols[/url]

Lua written by Claude Opus for this project.

[h1]Install / enable[/h1]
Subscribe, then enable the mod in the in-game Mod Manager. Safe to add to an existing save.
